# Exact lattice bridge from the E8+A2^3 target to X(6,79)

## Result and boundary

The three-section `II*+3I3+5I1` target is not merely another
determinant-948 construction.  Its exact finite quadratic form agrees with
the period lattice and transparent neighbor seed already attached to the
rank-17 `X(6,79)` K3 programme.

This proves a lattice-theoretic bridge.  It does **not** yet give an explicit
stable isometry, neighbor transformation, moduli map, rational `P3` section,
or elliptic curve of rank 31.

The executable certificate is

```bash
python3 research/certify_e8_a2_shimura_bridge.py \
  --compare certificates/e8_a2_shimura_bridge.json
```

It uses exact standard-library arithmetic only.

\[

## The target essential lattice

Write `F` for a fibre and `O` for the zero section.  For the three target
sections put

\[
Q_i=P_i-O-2F.
\]

Each `Q_i` is orthogonal to the hyperbolic plane generated by `O,F`.  From
`P_i^2=-2`, `P_i.O=0`, and `P_i.P_j=2`, one obtains

\[
-Q_i^2=4,\qquad -Q_i.Q_j=0\quad(i\ne j).
\]

Adjoin these vectors to the positive root lattice

\[
E_8\oplus A_2^{\oplus3}.
\]

The component orbit

```text
P1=(1,1,0), P2=(2,0,0), P3=(0,0,0)
```

adds precisely the pairings

```text
Q1-A2(1).component1 = -1
Q1-A2(2).component1 = -1
Q2-A2(1).component2 = -1.
```

The resulting integral even positive lattice `N_target` has

\[
\operatorname{rank}N_{target}=17,
\qquad \det N_{target}=948.
\]

Exact Fincke--Pohst enumeration finds 258 norm-two vectors, exactly

\[
240+3\cdot6,
\]

and none uses a `Q_i` coordinate.  Thus the root system is exactly
`E8+A2^3`, with a rank-three root complement as required.

## Finite quadratic form

The discriminant group of `N_target` is cyclic of order 948.  A displayed
dual generator has norm

\[
\frac{1709}{948}\pmod{2\mathbf Z}.
\]

The period lattice used by the existing `X(6,79)` neighbor workflow is

\[
T=
\begin{pmatrix}
-316&0&288\\
0&474&-15\\
288&-15&-262
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

It has signature `(2,1)`, determinant `-948`, cyclic discriminant group, and a
generator of norm `485/948 mod 2Z`.  The exact congruence

\[
1709\cdot67^2\equiv485\pmod{1896}
\]

proves that the finite quadratic forms are isometric.  The transparent
`A11+K6(det=79)` neighbor seed is independently joined by

\[
1589\cdot101^2\equiv485\pmod{1896}.
\]

Hence the new three-section target, the previous neighbor seed, and the
period lattice have the same exact discriminant-form fingerprint.

## Quaternion datum

An exact LDL diagonalization of `T` is

\[
\langle-316,474,1/158\rangle.
\]

For a ternary diagonal form `a,b,c`, the even Clifford algebra is represented
by `(-ab,-ac)`.  Here this reduces modulo squares to the quaternion algebra

\[
(6,2).
\]

Its Hilbert symbols are `-1` at 2 and 3, and `+1` at 79 and infinity.  Thus
the quaternion discriminant is 6, while the remaining squarefree level factor
is 79: exactly the `X(6,79)` datum from the primary sources.

## Consequence for the search

The most valuable next computation is no longer a blind four-parameter
search.  It is to construct a stable isometry or explicit neighbor chain from
`N_target` to the frozen determinant-948 chain, then transport the known
rational Shimura point through that chain into the two-split Weierstrass
coordinates.  Success would produce the missing characteristic-zero `P3`
section on the geometric rank-17 K3 source.  A later specialization search and
31-point independence certificate would still be required for the record.
